The Eboard, consisting of President, Vice President, Treasurer and Secretary, are in charge of directing all GrizzHacks operations. They orchestrate the goals and activities of our club, and plan ahead so others can focus on doing. While the exact roles of each might differ year to year based on the personalities and skillsets of each person on the board, *all of the responsibilities below should be covered to ensure a successful year*.

### President
The President lays out the overall plan for GrizzHacks and the goals for the team leads alongside the Vice President.

#### Major Responsibilities
1. Creating a yearlong timeline
2. Communicating with Jane, the Dean, the club advisor, the dean's board representee (Fred) and others
3. Leading weekly meetings
4. Ensuring the success of GrizzHacks

### Vice President
The Vice President assists the President in all matters, presiding over the club equally so that they can run GrizzHacks well next. The Vice President ideally graduates after the President for this reason.

### Treasurer
The treasurer handles the budget of GrizzHacks, and works closely with all teams to ensure enough money is coming in and a sustainable amount of money is going out. They are in charge of creating a master budgeting sheet and multiple plans based on varying amounts of money raised. They are the direct contact to Keith and Esther (who manage the SECS budget) and SAFB, and the only person to order all items (besides the President). They work together very closely with sponsorships and logistics.

The responsibility of the treasurer used to be that of an entire team (budgeting/procurement), but we've learned over time it works out much better to have one person handle all of these matters. As a result, the roles of the treasurer is *critical* to the success of GrizzHacks.

It is an SAFB rule that only the President and Treasurer can interact with the SAFB budget.

#### Major Responsibilities
1. Creating/Updating the Master Budgeting Form
2. Ensuring we have enough money
3. Procuring all items
4. Communicating all matters involving SECS or SAFB budget

### Secretary
The Secretary is in charge of making sure the club adheres to the goals outlined by the eboard. They are also the Plaky master (Plaky is our current Task Management System) and notetaker of meetings. Any problems teams are facing should be reported to the secretary, who will coordinate a solution. In a sense, the Secretary is the SCRUM Master of GrizzHacks.

#### Major Responsibilities
1. Master of Plaky
2. Ensure tasks are being completed on schedule
3. Removing impediments teams are facing
4. Taking notes during meetings.
5. Ensuring the organization of GrizzHacks
